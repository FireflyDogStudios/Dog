The layout

Like a video editor: the viewer in the middle, this inspector for the picked joint or track, and the timeline along the bottom. Each track (a leg, the head and back, or your own) twirls open into one row per joint with a diamond on every frame you placed it; the bar under the diamonds is a filled stretch, orange where it is unsure. Under each leg is its footfall row. Drag in the ruler to scrub; click a lane to pick that joint and frame. Track headers: ▾ open or close, double-click the name to rename, 👁 show or hide on the video, 🔒 lock against edits, S focus (solo: clicks, Fill, Auto adjust, copy and Clear stay inside it). Drag the bar above the timeline to resize it.

Modes

Manual (the default): only the points you place exist; Fill gaps fills the frames between from your points when you ask, and Filled points hides them again. Assisted: the frames between fill as you go. Auto adjust nudges your clicks up to 3 px to match the same spot on nearby frames; a dotted tick shows where you clicked and Revert adjust (or right-click) puts them back. Filled points come from matching each joint's look at your nearest points (never the frame before), the smoothest strong path, bones held near their length; ideas from Tracker, Kinovea, DLTdv, SLEAP and Anipose, our own code.

Where to click (bony points, not fur)

How exact is hand placing?

People clicking joints on video differ by about 2–3 pixels between tries and 2–3° on a joint angle, more near the knee where skin slides over bone.
