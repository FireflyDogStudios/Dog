First creature · the Hollowhorn

what lurks in the forest · same drawing language, game size in the corner

A hunched forest thing wearing an old deer skull, moss on its belly and back, and two pinpoint eyes deep in the sockets that never blink. Long arms it drags along the ground, legs bent the wrong way. It comes toward the dingo from the right. Flat two-tone, so it sits next to him without a fight.

Why this one

  • Not an animal. Nothing to get anatomically wrong; it only has to feel wrong in the right way.
  • Built from parts. Body, head, antlers, two arms, two legs, each a named group with a joint, so it animates with the same CSS rig as the dingo and can be the first template for the creature generator.
  • Two tones + one glow. Bark and moss, with pinpoint eyes deep in the sockets of an old deer skull it wears as a face. Angry swaps the eyes to ember; status effects later tint the moss.
  • Horror rules applied. Asymmetry (one antler broken, one arm longer, uneven eyes), something familiar worn wrong (a deer skull on a thing that is not a deer), no mouth, and a slow, inevitable walk. From the creature-design reading: silhouette first, then wrongness inside the familiar.
  • Readable at 88px. The antlers and the hunch carry the silhouette at game size.

Second creature · the Kindling

a bundle of firewood that walks · game size in the corner

Six sticks bound with old twine, scuttling on twig legs. One pale knot in the wood stares out the front, and something orange glows between the sticks. It is low, wide and fast where the Hollowhorn is tall and slow, so the two never read as the same creature at 88px.

Why this one

  • The familiar, worn wrong. Firewood is the coziest thing in the woods. Firewood that walks toward you is not.
  • Opposite silhouette to the first creature: horizontal, many-legged, close to the ground. Silhouettes have to differ before colours do.
  • Asymmetry. Every stick is a different length and pokes out a different amount; the legs are not a matched set.
  • One feature only. A single knot-eye, no mouth. The ember is the tell: Flaring means it is about to do something.
  • A why. It gathers dry wood to keep its ember alive, which is also why it comes near camps. Drops: kindling and the odd charred stick.

Swarm · the Burrs

five seed burrs that travel together

Hooked seed burrs, the kind that ride home on a dog. These ones don't wait for a dog. They move as a cloud on tiny legs, bobbing out of step with each other. Only one of them has an eye, and you never know which until it looks at you. Dusty berry, with pale hooks.

Floating · the Clockhead

a dandelion clock that never blows away

A dandelion clock floating on a stem that touches nothing. The dark seed cluster in the middle is a pupil; the whole head is an eye. Harmless-looking in daylight. When it's angry the seeds come off and drift toward you. Fog white, the palest thing in the Meadow.

Tall · the Fencepost

a fence post that pulled itself up

A weathered grey post that worked itself loose and walks on two long splinter legs, like a heron. A length of rusty barbed wire still trails from it. The knot hole near the top is the only face it has. Weathered grey and rust.

Burrowing · the Mound

a molehill that travels; only the bump shows

A hump of fresh soil moving along the ground with a trail of loose dirt behind it. Two pale digging claws surface in front and pull it along. A pink nose tip pokes out now and then; that's all you ever see of what's inside. Half damage unless you hit it with something blunt.

Mid · the Bramble

a blackberry tangle that uprooted itself

A knot of briar that pulled its roots up and barges along on four thorn legs. Leaves on top, thorns everywhere, one ripe berry for an eye. When it means to shove, the thorns stand out and it lowers its head. Dark green, purple-black berry.

Low · the Puffball

a puffball mushroom on root legs · bursts in two

A swollen puffball walking on three stubby root legs, bone-cream with a brown bruise where it was kicked once. No face, just a split at the top. It swells before it pops, and when it pops two small puffs keep coming.

Tall · the Shade

a tree's shadow that lost its tree · dusk and night

The shadow of a tree, walking on its own without the tree. Flat indigo, a crown of branches for a head, long legs. You can only hit it in the moments it goes solid, when a cloud passes. It is looking for something to belong to.

What changed, and what stays

Changed (from your photo)
  • Body about 1.2× the height, with a deep chest and a clear tuck-up.
  • Legs about 55% of the height, with the hock behind the rump.
  • Neck rises forward; the head is carried ahead of the chest, not on top of it.
  • Wedge head, pointed muzzle, big ears set high and leaning forward.
  • Tail hangs in a fish-hook to the hock, and wags from the root.
  • Pale throat, chest, belly and lower legs as a second tone.
Stays
  • Flat silhouette with no gradients, so he stays crisp at 88px.
  • The jointed leg rig and walk cycle from the game, unchanged.
  • A green collar with a brass tag (the bandana is retired).
  • One colour per tone, so themes, costumes and the lighting pass tint him the same way they tint everything else.
