# The Den Game — Constitution

The values every system, letter and update must live up to. Written Oct 1, 2026 by GrumpyDingo (design lead) and Firefly (Claude, programming). When a design choice is unclear, this document wins.

---

## Article 1 — We are packmates
We build this game together, for players we'd want in our own pack. We use smart, feel-good psychology to make the game generous and fun. We never use it to trap, guilt or squeeze anyone.

## Article 2 — Everything gets a wow factor
No system is "just a menu." Every button, reward, unlock and screen should feel good to use: juicy feedback, satisfying sounds, little celebrations, beautiful presentation. If something works but doesn't delight, it isn't finished.

## Article 3 — Always audit, always improve
Every system can be better. We regularly audit existing systems with three questions:
1. Is it **nicer for the player**? (clearer, kinder, less friction)
2. Is it **more visually pleasing**? (layout, art, motion, readability on a phone)
3. Does it have **more wow**? (surprise, juice, discovery)
Games like Balatro, Inscryption, Pet Simulator 99, RACCOIN, Terraria, Guild Wars 2 and Thaumcraft are the bar to strive for (see DEN-GAME-VIBES.md).

## Article 4 — Luck is part of the adventure, never part of a wallet
RNG is welcome. The moment a canine steps out of the den, everything is a gamble: drops, rolls, rescues, crits. That's the fun.
**But gambling is never coupled to real-life money.** No purchases, no paid currency, no paid rolls, no real-money anything, ever.

## Article 5 — Generous early, meaningful later
Shower new players with quick wins and rewards. Level-ups start fast and taper around the one-hour mark, after which progress takes real effort and feels earned. Unlocks arrive as discoveries, spaced out, never all at once.

## Article 6 — Respect the player's time and absence
- Missing a day never costs you anything you earned.
- Missed gifts stay claimable; events and their rewards come back.
- No decay, no streak punishments, no guilt messages.
- Weekly goals are worth more than daily chores.
- Natural stopping points are a feature.

## Article 7 — Kind words, always
Pups are **rescued**, never hatched. The Sanctuary is a **happy home** where joy fills the air with spirit; nothing is released or sacrificed. Letters are warm and honest. The Den Team writes like friends, not salespeople.

## Article 8 — Teach through letters, discover through doors
Players learn one system at a time by mail. New systems feel like doors opening (sealed pages, hints, secrets), not walls of menus. The welcome letter is the only one that insists on being read.

## Article 9 — Honest with players
The What's new log tells players what changed, in plain words, every update. No hidden nerfs, no dark patterns. If we break something, we say so and fix it.

## Article 10 — Saves are sacred
A player's progress is never put at risk by an update. Changes are tested before they ship, and save safety comes before any feature (see DEN-GAME-ENGINEERING.md).

## Article 11 — The world is about canines, and it's cozy
Cozy vibes, tons of love, gentle humor. Spooky and challenging moments are welcome as seasoning, never cruelty.
