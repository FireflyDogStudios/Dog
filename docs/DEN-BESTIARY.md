# The Complete Bestiary — All Animals & Species

Stored by Firefly on Oct 7, 2026, as GrumpyDingo gave it. **This is the current list of animals and species.** It supersedes the emoji enemy and pup rosters in the game code (placeholders for testing) and the creature bible `docs/claude/DEN-GAME-CREATURES.md`. Both are kept for the record. GrumpyDingo also said the Meadow creatures (Fencepost, Puffball and the rest) are **probably going to be deprecated** too; they are listed below as given until he decides.

Firefly's notes on facts and open points are at the end, kept separate from GrumpyDingo's text.

---

Organized by faction, with notes on role, canid status, and anything still open. I've included the Meadow creatures, notable individuals, and the open slots at the end.

## PART ONE: THE CANIDS

Everything canine-esque — dogs, wolves, foxes, hyenas, wild dogs, jackals, and the canid-adjacent. This is the core of the world.

### The Canid Family Tree (as the world knows it)

The dogs of this world don't use human taxonomy. They classify by scent-line, pack, and calling. But for your design purposes, here's the full spread:

### 1. THE HEARTHGUARD — the settled pack
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic dogs | Core population | The majority. Breed diversity is wide — terriers, hounds, shepherds, etc. Breed doesn't matter socially; den matters. |
| Coyotes | Core population | Smaller, cleverer, often Emberwrights or Quiet. Distrusted by Longpaws for being "too settled." |
| Jackals | Core population | The Hearthguard's oldest bloodline. Many Saltwardens are jackal-descended. |
| Golden jackals | Sub-population | A specific line within the Hearthguard. Traditionally the keepers of the Ledger. |
| Side-striped jackals | Sub-population | Rarer. Associated with the Quiet. |

Note: The Hearthguard does not include foxes, hyenas, or wild dogs. Those are "outsider species" — tolerated as traders, never as pack.

### 2. THE LONGPAWS — the wandering pack
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wolves | Core population | The largest and most respected. Redtooth leadership is traditionally wolf. |
| African wild dogs | Core population | The painted ones. Scent-Mappers and Far Howl are often wild dog. |
| Dingos | Core population | The player character is a Carolina Dog — a dingo-adjacent breed. Dingos are Longpaw-adjacent but often go their own way. |
| Carolina Dogs | Player species | The hero. Yellowish-brown, medium-sized, wary of strangers. The "dingo of the American South." |
| New Guinea singing dogs | Sub-population | Rare. Far Howl diplomats. Known for their eerie howl. |
| Coyotes | Tolerated | Longpaw-adjacent. Not fully trusted — "they settle too easy." |
| Jackals | Tolerated | Same as coyotes. |

Note: The Longpaws accept any canid who lives like a Longpaw. Wolves, wild dogs, and dingos are core. Coyotes and jackals are probationary. Foxes are not accepted.

### 3. THE UN-SCENTED — the outcast pack
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Red foxes | Large population | The most common Un-scented. Clever, adaptable, survivors. |
| Arctic foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Came from the cold north. Hollowhorn mystics. |
| Fennec foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Desert-origin. Nameless spies. |
| Gray foxes | Sub-population | Tree-climbers. Rag-and-Bone scavengers. |
| Hyenas | Significant population | Spotted, striped, and brown. Strong, social, not canids but canine-esque. The other factions treat them as Un-scented whether they like it or not. |
| Jackals | Common | Exiles from the Hearthguard, mostly. |
| Coyotes | Common | Exiles from the Longpaws, mostly. |
| Mixed-breeds | Very common | Dog × fox, dog × coyote, dog × jackal. The Un-scented don't care about lineage. |
| African wild dogs | Rare | Exiles from the Longpaws. Always tragic figures. |
| Dingos | Rare | Exiles. The player can be one. |

Note: The Un-scented are the only faction that accepts any canid-esque species. Foxes, hyenas, and mixed-breeds are all welcome. This is why they're the most diverse faction — and why the others distrust them.

### 4. THE TINKERS — the wildcard pack
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Raccoon dogs (tanuki) | Core population | The Tinkers' founders. Not true canids — they're vulpine-adjacent. Stocky, masked, clever. |
| Foxes | Common | Especially red foxes. The Tinkers and the Un-scented share fox blood. |
| Raccoons | Common | Not canids at all. The Tinkers don't care. They care about hands. |
| Corvids (crows, ravens, magpies) | Allied species | Not canids. Intelligent. The Tinkers' scouts and messengers. |
| Badgers | Rare | Borrowed from the caves. Diggers. Kettleworks specialists. |
| Otters | Rare | River-scavengers. Brass-work specialists. |

Note: The Tinkers are the only faction that includes non-canids as full members. This is why the other factions consider them weird. The Tinkers consider the other factions limited.

### 5. THE TALLOW — the cynical faction
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any | Core population | The Tallow recruits from all species. They don't care what you are. They care what you'll do. |
| Red foxes | Overrepresented | Many Tallow are former Un-scented foxes who went too far. |
| Hyenas | Overrepresented | The Tallow's philosophy appeals to hyenas. "You already eat the dead" resonates. |
| Mixed-breeds | Overrepresented | The exiled, the nameless, the ones nobody claimed. |
| Domestic dogs | Present | Exiles from the Hearthguard. The Tallow's best recruiters. |
| Wolves | Rare | Exiles from the Longpaws. The Tallow's best hunters. |

Note: The Tallow has no species identity. That's the point. They are what happens when species stops mattering and only hunger remains.

### 6. THE VERMILION CONCORD — the fox faction
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Red foxes | Core population | The Concord's founding species. Almost all foxes are red foxes. |
| Arctic foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Purists, mostly. The cold-blooded wing. |
| Gray foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Luminaries, mostly. The curious wing. |
| Fennec foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Watchers, mostly. The patient wing. |
| Swift foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Open Door, mostly. The friendly wing. |
| Kit foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Researchers. |
| Corsac foxes | Sub-population | Rare. Archivists. |

Note: The Concord is almost exclusively fox. A non-fox can become an ambassador to the Concord, but cannot become a member. The foxes are polite about this. They are also absolute.

### 7. THE ELDERS — the old world
| Species | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unknown | Precursor species | Resembled canids. Were not canids. Built the ruins. Had Calling. Vanished at the Silence. |

Open questions:
- Were they a canid species that evolved differently?
- Were they a non-canid species that looked like canids?
- Were they the first dogs, changed by time and Calling?
- Were they something else entirely?

Recommended: Non-canid beings who resembled canids. This makes the Silence a loss of contact rather than a loss of family, which is scarier.

## PART SIX: THE PLAYER'S SPECIES

The player is a Carolina Dog.
- Also called: American Dingo, Yellow Dog, Carolina Yellow Dog.
- Appearance: Medium-sized, yellowish-brown, pointed ears, long snout, wolf-like but smaller.
- Temperament: Wary of strangers, deeply loyal to pack, adaptable, survivor.
- Why it fits: Carolina Dogs are dingo-adjacent — they're a naturally occurring breed, not a designed one. They're what happens when dogs go feral and stay feral. They're the perfect species for a hero who belongs to no faction and has to find their own way.

The player's companion is also a dog. Species open, but I'd recommend a mixed-breed — something that visually contrasts with the Carolina Dog. A black-and-white terrier mix, a brindle hound, a scruffy little thing. The companion is the heart of the game, and their species should reflect that.

Starting Pack options: Longpaws or Un-scented. The player begins outside the civilized center. The Hearthguard is the faction you visit and don't fully trust.

## Summary

You have:
- 7 factions — Hearthguard, Longpaws, Un-scented, Tinkers, Tallow, Vermilion Concord, Elders
- ~30 canid species across the factions, from domestic dogs to foxes to hyenas to raccoon dogs
- 9 meadow creatures (plus 3 open slots)
- 6+ non-canid species (prey, predators, allies)
- 6 named individuals
- 1 player species (Carolina Dog)
- 1 companion (species open)
- Multiple open slots for future expansion

The world is dense now. Every faction has a species identity, every species has a faction home, and every faction has a philosophical position on the Meat and Bone Question.

## Firefly's notes (Oct 7; facts only, not changes to the text above)
- **Raccoon dogs are true canids.** The raccoon dog (*Nyctereutes procyonoides*) is in the dog family, Canidae, an old branch near the foxes. Raccoons (Procyonidae), badgers and otters (Mustelidae) are not canids, and hyenas (Hyaenidae) are closer to cats. The in-world line can stay as the characters believe it; GrumpyDingo decides.
- **Side-striped jackals** belong with the black-backed jackal in their own genus (*Lupulella*), more distant from the golden jackal (*Canis aureus*) than wolves are. A nice fit for "rarer, associated with the Quiet".
- **Species the species creator would need** (real anatomy), by skull type: wolf-type (wolf, coyote, golden jackal), side-striped jackal, African wild dog, dingo / Carolina Dog / New Guinea singing dog, domestic breeds (vary most), foxes (red, arctic, fennec, swift, kit, corsac; gray fox has its own skull), raccoon dog, hyenas (spotted, striped, brown), then non-canids (raccoon, badger, otter, deer, rabbit and hare, rodents, boar, snake; corvids and other birds need a bird skeleton).
