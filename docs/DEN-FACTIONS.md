# Den factions

Stored by Firefly on Oct 6, 2026, as GrumpyDingo gave it. **The goal (GrumpyDingo):** armor and other pieces will eventually be based on each faction, so the game avoids the "10,000 bowls of oatmeal" problem, where lots of items exist but they all look the same. Each faction becomes a style vocabulary in the sense of `DEN-GEAR-DESIGN-GUIDE.md`: its own shape language, signature break, edge profile, gem cut and plate rhythm, applied to every slot and any material.

The bracketed numbers are the footnote markers of the original. Their source list was not part of what was shared, so they point nowhere yet.

## 1. The Hearthguard
- **Residing animals:** domestic dogs, coyotes and jackals [1].
- **Weapons:** heavy, durable weapons crafted from oak and ember-forged iron, including short swords with wide blades, leather-wrapped bone clubs, weighted tug-ropes and shovels [1][2]. They respect repaired weapons with history more than brand-new ones [2].

## 2. The Longpaws
- **Residing animals:** wolves, African wild dogs and dingos [3].
- **Weapons:** ranged and thrown weapons designed to keep hunters mobile, such as slingshots, throwing discs, spears and bows [3][4]. Crafted from light wood, sinew, gut-string and flint tips; their Called weapons hover further out at the edge of their reach [4].

## 3. The Un-scented
- **Residing animals:** foxes, hyenas, jackals, mixed-breeds, and exiles from other packs [5].
- **Weapons:** mismatched, re-forged composite weapons, such as broken swords, re-forged blades, salvaged spearheads and sharpened bone [5][6]. They combine parts from multiple factions (for example a Hearthguard blade on a Longpaw haft), so their Called weapons hover irregularly at different heights and distances [6].

## 4. The Tinkers
- **Residing animals:** non-pure canine species, including raccoon-dogs, tanuki-adjacent species, foxes, and allied corvids [7].
- **Weapons:** clockwork, steam-powered and gear-driven mechanisms, including repeating slingshots, pressurized bone-lances and vibrating swords [8]. Made from brass, copper, salvaged steel and unknown old-world alloys; their Called weapons jitter, spark and steam [8].

## 5. The Tallow
- **Residing animals:** members recruited from any species, or pack exiles [9].
- **Weapons:** flaying knives, cleavers and hooks carved exclusively from harvested bone rather than mined or forged metal [9][10]. Their Called weapons hover with smooth, motionless precision [10].

## 6. The Vermilion Concord
- **Residing animals:** almost exclusively foxes [11].
- **Weapons:** elegant, precision-engineered weapons made of steel, silver and brass, including rapiers, thin blades, throwing needles, and fans with hidden edges [12]. Their Called weapons hover in symmetrical, choreographed formation [12].

## Where this goes next (not started)

- The current pieces (Hide armor, wraps, cuffs, bracelets, hood) are the plain base that faction styles theme-stack on.
- Once the basic pieces are settled, the design guide and these factions become a skill.
