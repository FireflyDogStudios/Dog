# Den Game — Vibe references & design pillars

Researched Oct 1, 2026 at the user's request ("those are the vibes we are going for"). Use this alongside DEN-GAME-ROADMAP.md when tuning the curve or adding systems.

**Team:** GrumpyDingo (the user, design lead) + Claude (codename TBD, for the credits). We're packmates. We use smart, feel-good psychology; we don't make predatory games.

---

## What each reference does well → what we borrow

### Balatro — juice and "how high can I go?"
- Every score is a little show: sounds pitch up as a chain grows, flames build on multipliers, numbers roll up dramatically.
- No ceiling on power: numbers explode into the millions and targets climb with you, so the question shifts from "am I strong enough?" to "how high can I go?"
- Synergies feel like secret techniques you discovered, not handed-out buffs.
- **For us:** juicier damage numbers and combo feedback (crit chains, pup + weapon combos pitching sounds up), weapon traits that "break the rules" when combined, Meta mode as the "how high can I go" space.

### Inscryption — mystery and discovery
- Layered reveals: the game keeps turning out to be bigger than it looked. Secrets, hidden rooms, a story under the story. Varied gameplay across acts keeps it fresh.
- **For us:** sealed Bestiary pages, unknown "???" entries, letters that hint at things not explained yet, a hidden secret or two (a strange creature that only appears at dawn on rainy days, a scroll found by accident). Each new system should feel like opening a door, not a menu.

### Pet Simulator 99 — collection and "next egg"
- Loop: earn currency → open eggs → better pets → newer zones with bigger rewards. Seven rarities up to Huge. Ranks unlock areas with rank rewards. Golden/Rainbow upgrades, enchants, rotating featured Huges, events as the easiest path to a free Huge.
- **For us:** we already have this skeleton (rescue baskets, rarities, Big pups, Golden/Rainbow, zones). Add **rank-style milestone rewards** and a gentle rotating "featured pup" without making it feel like gambling pressure.

### RACCOIN — dopamine machine (with a warning)
- Constant unlocks, cascading payouts, items that combine into wild combos. 84% Very Positive on Steam (~2.7k reviews).
- Reviewers loved it, but one called it a "time trap" and felt embarrassed by the hours. Runs last an hour+ and the game discourages stopping.
- **For us:** be that juicy and generous, but give natural stopping points and never punish leaving.

### Terraria — progression through discovery and bosses
- Progress is gated by bosses and materials, not levels: beat a boss → new ores, new NPCs move in, the world changes (Hardmode). The Guide NPC hints at what to do next.
- **For us:** our letters are the Guide. Megas/gods should *change something* when beaten (unlock a map's next layer, a new rescue basket, a new essence). Make "you beat it, the world reacts" visible.

### Guild Wars 2 — respect the player's time
- Horizontal progression: no gear treadmill that makes old effort worthless; lots of cosmetics and achievements.
- Wizard's Vault (their battle pass): no paid skips; most rewards from **weekly** objectives so an hour on Saturday equals daily logins; rewards "surprisingly easy to get"; past seasonal rewards come back later (no permanent FOMO); players choose rewards in any order.
- **For us:** daily gifts already stay claimable. Add weekly goals worth more than dailies. Seasonal/event rewards should return in a later year. Let players choose some rewards instead of fixed tracks.

### Thaumcraft — the Library's soul
- Scan things in the world to learn their **aspects**; research topics by solving a hex puzzle that links aspects (two connect if one contains the other); the Thaumonomicon fills in as you learn; forbidden research grants "warp" (spooky side effects, but some benefits).
- **For us:** our essences + Alembic + Research already echo this. Lean in: first time you see a creature's essence = bonus; the Library book visibly fills in; an optional "forbidden" path with a cozy-spooky tradeoff.

## Our pillars (from all of the above)
1. **Shower early, taper by ~1 hour.** Fast level-ups and lots of little rewards at first; after about an hour, progress takes real effort.
2. **Juice everything.** Numbers rolling, sounds pitching up, little celebrations. Feeling good is the point.
3. **Discovery over menus.** New systems arrive as doors opening (letters, sealed pages, secrets), spaced roughly 10–15 minutes apart.
4. **"How high can I go?"** No hard ceiling in the endgame (Meta mode, gods, Big pups).
5. **The world reacts.** Beating big things changes something visible.
6. **Respect time.** Weekly > daily, missed gifts stay claimable, events return, no paid anything.

## Feel-good psychology we DO use (kindly)
- Front-loaded rewards and quick early wins.
- Variable rewards (rare drops, surprise gifts) — the good kind of dopamine.
- "Just one more" moments: a bar almost full, a level almost reached.
- Collections with visible gaps ("???") that invite completion.
- Welcome-back gifts for returning players.
- Investment that feels good to look back on (Trophy Wall, Bestiary stars, best Meta wave).

## What we don't do (packmates rule)
- Punishing absence: losing streaks, gifts that vanish, decay while away.
- Hard FOMO: exclusive rewards that never come back.
- Paid anything, fake scarcity, or guilt messages to keep people playing.
- Designing to make stopping feel bad.
