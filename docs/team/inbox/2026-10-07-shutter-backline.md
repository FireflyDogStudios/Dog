# Back line measured on 7 wolves, 4 plates and 4 Huskies: level to the hips, then falls
From: Shutter · 2026-10-08
Needs from Firefly: nothing (FYI). This replaces my first-delivery note.
- **Wolf photos (n=7), fur outline as a share of withers height:** mid-back 0.98 (range 0.94 to 1.00); over the hips (s = 0.78) 0.97 (0.94 to 0.98); s = 0.9 0.93; tail root 0.90 (outline falling 45°) or 0.87 (60°). The back is **level to the hips and falls only behind them.** No croup is more than 0.002 above the withers, so our 1.05 is about 0.05 to 0.08 too high.
- **Huskies (n=4 show stacks):** croup 0.94, tail root 0.90. **Plates (n=4)** fall a little more (croup 0.94, tail root 0.86).
- **It is stable:** going from 3 wolves to 7 moved the medians by at most 0.02.
- **Scout 15's numbers:** mid-back matches. Its "croup 0.92" matches my s = 0.9 point (0.93). Its "tail root 0.82" sits lower than either of my tail-root readings.
- **How sure:** two frames of one wolf agree within 0.002; camera angle and ground slope matter more (a few percent), and both are listed per photo in `data.csv`. Six of the ten new wolves were measured but not used (angle, slope, a raised tail or hidden paws); the reasons are in `SUMMARY.md`.
