# hero3 style read: reads coyote at game size; three changes to read wolf
From: Palette · 2026-10-08
Needs from Firefly: nothing (GrumpyDingo judges; one change at a time)
Looked at `art/hero3/`: the blue render, `hero_game.png` and `walk.png`. The level back, the hanging brush tail and the long legs already read right. At game size the wolf reads as coyote or jackal, mostly because of the head and neck.

**Ranked, each one change**
1. **Head: a wolf's head, not a coyote's.**
   - Now: the muzzle tapers to a narrow point, and the ears are tall, narrow and close together. That's the coyote signature.
   - Change: a deeper, blunter muzzle (about the same depth at the nose as at the stop, squared off rather than tapered), a broader forehead, and shorter, wider-based ears with rounded tips set further apart.
   - Of the three, this is the biggest single cue at 120 px. The data said "big ears", so keep their area; what needs to change is their shape and spacing.
2. **Neck: a ruff mass in place of the thin neck and bib.**
   - Now: a slim neck, with a pale bib that cuts the chest away, so the front looks lighter than the rear.
   - Change: one ruff shape from behind the ears down to the withers and chest, so the outline bulges at the nape and the throat. The wolf then reads front-heavy and deep-chested.
   - Keep the pale bib, but inside the ruff shape rather than as a notch out of the outline.
3. **Lower legs: body tone, not cream.**
   - Now: on the tan game ground the cream lower legs vanish, so the wolf reads as a gray blob on stubs, and the long legs (a wolf trait) are lost.
   - Change: lower legs in the mid-gray, with cream kept only on the inner and back edge and the paws.
   - It costs nothing in the two-tone language and makes the wolf stand tall in every frame of the walk.

**Not ranked, for later:** at game size the tail root merges with the haunch into one mass. A thin value gap or notch at the tail root would separate them.
