# Sources and licences of the files in this folder

Coordinates only, no images. Use for ratios and pose ranges, never for tracing (original art only). Credit the source in the game's `CREDITS` when something built from it ships.

| File | Source | Licence | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `badja_dogs.json` | [BADJA](https://github.com/benjiebob/BADJA), `joint_annotations/{dog,dog-agility,rs_dog}.json` (Biggs et al. 2018, "Creatures great and SMAL") | MIT, see `LICENSE-BADJA.txt` | 3 dog video sequences (12, 5 and 201 annotated frames). `{sequence: [{img, kp:{name:[x,y]}}]}`. Converted from BADJA's (row, col) to (x, y). Names: SMAL joints 8-10 / 12-14 = front leg elbow, wrist, paw (`front_{right,left}_{thai,knee,paw}`, AwA-style names); 18-20 / 22-24 = hind stifle, hock, paw (`back_*`); 15 `neck_lower`; 25/28/31 `tail_base`/`tail_mid`/`tail_end`; 32 `jaw`; 33 `nose`; 35/36 `right_eartip`/`left_eartip` (no ear base). Only visible joints kept. |
| `stanfordextra_sample.json` | [StanfordExtra](https://github.com/benjiebob/StanfordExtra) `StanfordExtra_sample.json` (Biggs et al. 2020) | MIT (the dataset was relicensed MIT on 2 Nov 2024), see `LICENSE-StanfordExtra.txt` | The 25 sample dogs (Rhodesian ridgeback, Weimaraner, Border terrier, golden retriever), `{breed: [{img, kp}]}`, pixel coordinates, AwA-style names (`front_left_thai` = elbow, `front_left_knee` = wrist, `back_*_thai` = top of hind leg (about the stifle), `back_*_knee` = hock), plus `tail_base`, `tail_end`, `left/right_earbase`, `left/right_earend`, `nose`, `chin`. |
| `proportions.json` | computed here (see REPORT.md) | facts derived from the above, AwA-Pose (MIT) and the StanfordExtra annotations (see below) | All medians, quartiles and counts behind REPORT.md. |
| `stanfordextra_breeds_unitB.csv` | **regenerated Oct 7 by Atlas** from the MIT StanfordExtra v12 data in `../fetched/02-outline-landmarks/data.json` with `tools/atlas/regen_unitB.py` (method of REPORT.md section 3; the script reproduces the stored AwA unit B numbers exactly) | MIT (StanfordExtra) | Per-breed table, 120 breeds + ALL_DOMESTIC (1,701 profile dogs), now including dingo (5) and dhole (15). Replaces the earlier 112-breed table built from the AGPL-labelled Ultralytics mirror (approval A-002 item 3). |
