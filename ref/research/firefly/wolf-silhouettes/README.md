# Wolf silhouettes (Oct 9, 2026)

Black-on-white cut-outs of the Scout's licensed wolf photos (`ref/research/scout/01-body-templates/wolf/`), each turned to face right and cropped to the animal. Made by `tools/den/wolf_silhouettes.py` (IS-Net via rembg, the same cutter as `den outline --photo`).

Only the clean cuts are kept: 01 (Rob Foster, CC BY 4.0), 02, 04 and 08 (J. G. Keulemans 1890, public domain), 05 (Drew Avery, CC BY 2.0), 10 (Sean Frey, CC BY 4.0). Grass, bushes or blur merged into 07, 09, 11 and 12, so those were dropped; `contact-sheet.png` still shows all ten tries. Full credits: `ref/research/scout/01-body-templates/candidates.csv`.

GrumpyDingo offered to cut the photos by hand (more accurate at the ears, tail and paws); those will replace these when they arrive. `tools/den/wolf_overlay.py` (draft, not run yet) lines the silhouettes up into a "mean wolf" and overlays hero3 on it.
