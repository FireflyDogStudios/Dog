Changes made (CC BY requires indicating changes): the original STL scans were
reoriented into an anatomical frame, the mandible of 170753 was hinged 2 degrees and the
mandible of 170555 was repositioned into occlusion (fitted), the meshes were decimated
(quadric edge collapse) to 140,000 (skull) and 90,000 (mandible) faces and saved as GLB,
and measurements, landmarks and outlines were derived from them. These derived files
are not endorsed by the Mammal Research Institute. Details: NOTE.md.
