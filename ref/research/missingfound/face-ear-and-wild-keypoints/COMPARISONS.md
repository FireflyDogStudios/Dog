# Ears, jaws and keypoint datasets: pasted notes checked (2026-10-07)

**Read this first.** GrumpyDingo pasted research notes written by another AI assistant. They came with **no sources**. Each claim below was checked against a real paper, page or licence file. Use only the parts marked confirmed or partly confirmed, in their corrected form. Anything marked unsupported or wrong should not go into the game or the species files as a fact.

Labels used in the tables:
- **Measured:** a number measured on that species.
- **Comparison:** a number measured on a related species.
- **Guess:** Firefly's suggestion for the game, with the reasoning. It is not a fact.

New numbers are also in `numbers.json` under `verified_oct7_comparisons`. Each has `kind` (measured / comparison / guess) and a confidence grade (A / B / C).

Licence rules follow `docs/claude/DEN-DATA-FETCH-PROMPT.md`. From non-commercial (NC) or paywalled papers, only single facts are cited. Nothing is copied in bulk.

## Jaw gape by species

Angles are between the upper and lower jaw.

**Two measurement scales disagree, so use ratios.** Andersson 2011 Table S1 measured skulls at "maximum extension" (the angle at the jaw joint to the incisor tips). It reads much lower than the live or bony-limit studies:
- *Canis* 27° there, against 44 ± 4° in live dogs (Thomson 2021);
- *Panthera* 47° there, against 65-77° bony gape (Deutsch 2025).

So Andersson's values are used only as **ratios between species**, scaled onto the live dog value of 44°.

Red fox cadaver and MRI about 80-84° (repo `factcheck/`, Penrose et al. 2020). | **Max 44**, hard limit 65. **Yawn** 40-44 (DogFACS AU27 stretch, held ≥ 1 s). **Bark** 15-25, opening and closing at about 2 per s (Déaux 2024). **Howl** 15-20 (no data; Ekström 2024 says howl jaw opening is undescribed). **Pant** 10-20 (AU26 relaxed drop plus tongue). **Whine** 0-5: a dog whines with its mouth closed (cineradiography, cited by Frey 2016). **Bite pose** 25-35 (Bourke). |
