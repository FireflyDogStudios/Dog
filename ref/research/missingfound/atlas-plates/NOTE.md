# Ellenberger-Baum dog plates (Tafel 1 and Tafel 3)

Added Oct 7, 2026 at Firefly's request (the UW library site is blocked from Firefly's session).

- **Source:** Ellenberger, Baum, Dittrich & Münch, *Handbuch der Anatomie der Tiere für Künstler*, dog volume (*Anatomie des Hundes*, 1st edition, Leipzig, ca. 1911-25). University of Wisconsin-Madison Digital Collections:
  - Tafel 3 (skeleton inside the body outline): https://search.library.wisc.edu/digital/AHFYCAGUWKQA5Y83 (IIIF: `https://asset.library.wisc.edu/iiif/1711.dl%2F5CIIJ6SEYKWOG8S/full/full/0/default.jpg`)
  - Tafel 1 (exterior): https://search.library.wisc.edu/digital/ATC7FOZKECJ5FE9D (IIIF ID TUNHELIQZJOUV8J)
- **Rights:** the library page states **"No known copyright"** (rightsstatements.org/vocab/NKC/1.0/), checked Oct 7, 2026. Published before 1931, so public domain in the US; Wikimedia Commons carries the same Tafel 3 as PD-old. No credit is legally required.
- **Files:** `tafel3_skeleton_left-lateral.jpg` (3451 × 2850, greyscale, 1.1 MB) and `tafel1_exterior_left-lateral.jpg` (3451 × 2850, 1.3 MB). Unmodified full-resolution downloads from the IIIF server (Oct 6). The dog faces **left**; the UW perforation stamp is visible.
- **These are the exact images the measurements in `ref/research/fetched/01-skin-offsets/data.json` were taken from**, so its pixel coordinates (left-facing originals) apply directly. Tafel 1 x = Tafel 3 x + 31 px. Withers height on Tafel 3 = 1837 px.
