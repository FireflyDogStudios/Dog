# Wolf outline proportions from Scout's templates (Firefly, Oct 7, 2026)

**What:** visible outline points read by eye from gridded previews of Scout's standing side-view wolves (`ref/research/scout/01-body-templates/wolf/`; licences in its `candidates.csv`: CC BY photos, public-domain plates). Points: withers top (fur surface above the front legs), ground (paw line, front legs), chest front (point of shoulder), buttock (rear of the rump, above the tail), chest floor (lowest chest line behind the elbow), nose tip. All ratios are shares of the withers-to-ground height measured the same way, so they compare with the 3D skeleton's outline points (bone + skin + fur offsets).

**Reading error:** about ±10 px on previews of 330–380 px height, so ±0.03 on each ratio. Plates are artists' drawings (Keulemans 1890): proportions are informative but heads tend to be raised or stretched. Left out: 06 and 13 (heads turned), 07 (feet hidden in grass), 10–12 (walking), 14 (three-quarter view).
