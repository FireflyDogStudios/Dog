# Outline methods: how others turn skeletons into silhouettes and heads (Oct 7, 2026)

Research by a Firefly research agent; saved by Firefly from its hand-back. **Access caveat:** the proxy blocked almost every publisher and data host (only github.com fetched), so facts come from search snippets; licences marked "verify" are unconfirmed. Nothing new was downloaded.

## Why outline v1 goes wrong
- **Lumpy:** a tight concave hull plus small smoothing follows every bone bump; muscles are straight thickened lines of action (real muscles are bellies; deep muscles never reach the skin); one median skin offset ignores the fascia and fat that bridge hollows.
- **Trunk stretch:** partly units. Stark's withers height is taken in a non-standing default pose (carpus over-extended 37°), and ours is 707 mm vs 750 field. Fix: map the trunk in mm with its own depth scale.
- **Skull:** an affine map keeps parallel lines parallel, so it keeps the Beagle's dome, stop and short deep muzzle; it can only shear them. Eye, stop, jaw joint and ear base stay where the Beagle has them.

## Recommendation: warp a real template, demote bones and muscles to checks
Geometric morphometrics, forensic facial reconstruction (Quatrehomme 1997), the Mesolithic Muge dog head (Applied Sciences 2022), Anatomy Transfer (Ali-Hamadi/Dicko et al. 2013) and VFX skin layers (Weta Tissue, Ziva) all **warp a real template that already has skin onto the target's landmarks**; the skin never traces each muscle.
1. **Template:** digitise the full outline of Ellenberger Tafel 3 (public domain; skeleton drawn inside the body outline on one plate, so bone and skin are registered): ~150 outline points plus every visible bone landmark (17 already in `fetched/01-skin-offsets`).
2. **Optional fur-level check:** GPA mean of StanfordExtra profile outlines (Ibizan hound 47, elkhound 31, basenji 26, malamute 25, dhole 23, husky 14, German shepherd 14; dingo only 1).
3. **Warp per region:** trunk and neck by thin-plate spline on withers, T1, croup, ischium, sternum, brisket, point of shoulder, elbow, stifle, tail base (+ wolf photo ratios as skin targets); legs by the existing per-bone maps, blended at joints (the same skinning the game will animate with); head on its own. Space points ≤ 0.005 WH; check validity; report TPS bending energy as "distance from template".
4. **Stark bones and muscles become a check layer:** every bone landmark must sit inside the outline at its Ellenberger offset.
5. **If grow-over-bones stays as a fallback:** superficial muscles only, as fusiform bellies; a convolution field (Gaussian kernels contoured, Spore's metaball idea) or a 0.04–0.06 WH morphological closing instead of the hull; skin offsets interpolated between landmarks; a low-harmonic elliptic Fourier or spline finish.
6. **Artists agree** (Ellenberger, Goldfinger, Hultgren, Blaise): three masses (skull, ribcage deepest just behind the elbow, pelvis) joined by neck and loin curves; bones pin the line only at surface landmarks (withers, point of shoulder, olecranon, accessory carpal, tuber coxae, ischium, trochanter, patella, calcaneus, zygomatic arch, occipital crest); long smooth curves between.

## Skull plan
- **Wolf vs dog profile:** flatter stop; long narrow muzzle; lateral orbital angle 39–46° in wolves vs 49–55° in dogs (below 35 always wolf, above 60 always dog); strong sagittal and occipital crests, big zygomatic arches, large carnassials and canines, deep mandible. CBL males 238.9 ± 10.7 mm, females 227.2 ± 10.4 (our file uses Law's 253.5 from 2 males).
- **S1 real wolf skulls** (need a human download; verify each licence): Sketchfab "Wolf Skull FW2415" (Oregon State), "Canis lupus (Wilk szary)" (Mammal Research Institute PAS), tylercwilson "Wolf Skull", listed as CC BY; CC0 check-only backup: Smithsonian 3D *Canis dirus*. Rejected (non-commercial or no-derivatives): Idaho Virtualization Lab, VMNH/VCU, zmmu, MorphoMuseuM, likely U Wyoming; Phenome10K and MorphoSource Yellowstone wolves unverified. Possible PD plates: Young & Goldman 1944.
- **S2** orthographic left lateral render (jaw closed, palate horizontal; Blender headless or trimesh); outline + ~20 landmarks (prosthion, rhinion, nasion, postorbital process, bregma, crest high point, inion, occipital condyle, basion, TMJ, zygomatic arch top/bottom, orbit rim front/back/centre, infraorbital foramen, P4, infradentale, chin, mandible border at M1, angular process, coronoid); store numbers only, in CBL units.
- **S3** with 3+ skulls, GPA mean (morphops), scale to CBL, check orbital angle and stop.
- **S4 skull-first head:** eye, stop, TMJ, ear base, nose, chin, occiput come from the skull template; similarity maps only, never affine; the mandible is its own body hinged at the TMJ.
- **S5 soft tissue:** TPS-warp the Tafel 3 head (skull + skin on one plate) onto the wolf skull; temporalis fills the head line smoothly (crest not visible), masseter fills the cheek, thin skin on the nasal dorsum; Ellenberger offsets skull top ~0.017 WH, occiput ~0.008, chin ~0.012; ear base just behind and above the ear canal; eye inside the orbit on a slant following the low orbital angle; cheek ruff later.
