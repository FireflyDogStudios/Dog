# CC BY-SA images worth looking at: links only (Shutter, Oct 7, 2026)

**Rule (GrumpyDingo asked for best practice; Firefly advised this, Oct 7):** CC BY-SA images stay **out of the repo**. Nothing here is downloaded or copied; these are links to look at. If a CC BY-SA photo is used to help build something that ships, ask first.

**Why a list at all:** these are the best side-on gaiting shots, the best hairless show stacks, and a few front and top views. Each line is a look-up, not a source. The search links open Wikimedia Commons results for a file-name pattern.
