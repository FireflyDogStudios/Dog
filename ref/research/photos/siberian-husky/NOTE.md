# Siberian Husky reference photos: search notes

Collected 2026-10-07 by the Photographer sub-agent. Every kept file is listed in `catalogue.csv` with its source page, author, licence and notes.
I checked the licence on each file's own page: the Flickr photo page (`"license":N` in the page JSON) or the Commons file page (licence template).

  canicross husky, bikejoring husky, husky jogging, dogs day out husky, husky dog park.
- **Openverse** (by/cc0/pdm, about 220 results): siberian husky (wikimedia), husky dog show, siberian husky standing/show,
  husky side view, sibirischer husky, husky gaiting, husky running, siberian husky trotting, husky walking, sled dog trotting.
- **Wikimedia Commons**: crawled Category:Siberian_Husky to depth 2 (668 files; 509 screened after dropping the hybrid, puppy, art and
  historic-dog subcategories). That covers Huskies in Russia, Germany, the US and the Philippines, Siberian Husky heads, and the named dogs Ivan, Balder and Leïko.
  I opened 82 promising file pages to check their licences.
- **iNaturalist**: Canis familiaris with q=husky or q=siberian husky and licence cc-by/cc0 returned only 12 photos. None were side-on and of usable quality.
- **NPS Denali**: the Denali kennel dogs that turned up in the sled-dog searches are **Alaskan huskies (working mixes), not
  Siberian Huskies**, so I did not use them for this breed. No US-federal Siberian side views were found.

### Breed or quality
- Flickr 3134784310 / 3134782724 (Wonderlane, CC BY 2.0) are titled **Alaskan Malamute**. They are side views in the snow, and the Malamute agent may want them.
- Flickr 3414721108 "Deisel" (CC BY 2.0) is a good walking side view, but the breed is unstated and it looks like a mix.
- Commons Anadyr.JPG (PD, 800 px) is a Chukotka sled-type dog of uncertain breed. Perros_antárticos3.JPG (PD) is a 3/4 view of an Antarctic sled dog of uncertain breed.

- Many of the remaining candidates were rejected as 3/4 or frontal views, puppies, harness teams seen head-on, or too small.

## Blocked / limits
- Commons originals (upload.wikimedia.org) return HTTP 429. The Commons files here are the largest cached thumbnail that loaded (1920/1280/960 px); see the notes per file.
  Thumbnails at or near full width (for Benskili, Siberian_Huskies_racing, Anadyr, thor) returned 400 errors.
- Flickr static images rate-limited (HTTP 429) towards the end. These were not downloaded:
  55451190009 (PeCeT_full, CC0, a show dog head in true side profile, 6,240 px; **recommended**),
  2321855836 (Pamela Carls, CC BY 2.0, head), 6860871299 (Taro the Shiba Inu, CC BY 2.0, a husky trotting beside other dogs) and
  11293289843 (Stephen Bowler, CC BY 2.0, standing by a lake). All four licences were already verified on the photo pages.
- Nothing needed a login or a permission email.

## Gaps
- **Trotting side-on** (show-ring gaiting) under a clean licence is scarce. The good examples are all CC BY-SA (see above).
- True side-on **sitting** was not found.

## Shutter's compliance review (Oct 7)
- I re-checked every licence on its source page: 19 Flickr pages and 6 Commons pages. All matched.
- **Removed:** standing 10 (Jack Berry, Flickr 3632247277). It is a scan of a print from the 2000 Manchester dog show, so the photographer may have been a show photographer and not the uploader. The authorship is unclear, and the photo was low-res anyway. Standing ranks 01–09 stand.
- **Flagged:** head 04 (Frank Nelissen) carries a Public Domain Mark the photographer applied to his own photo. That is a statement, not a CC0 waiver, so it is kept and flagged.
- **Corrected:** walking 01 (David Chau) is a front three-quarter view, not side-on. Walking 02 (Gnilenkov): the breed is doubtful. Both are noted in the catalogue.
- **So there is no clean side-on walking husky yet.** Trotting is 1 (low-res). The best fixes are the CC BY-SA Commons gaiting shots listed above (permission needed), or a retry of the four Flickr downloads that hit HTTP 429.
