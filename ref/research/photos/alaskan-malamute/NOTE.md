# Alaskan Malamute reference photos: search notes

Searched 2026-10-07. 14 images kept: standing 6, walking 1, trotting 0, head 4, lying 3, sitting 0.
Licence, author and size for each file are in `catalogue.csv`.
**The open-licence pool for this breed is small.** In particular, no clean side-on trotting or gaiting photo exists under CC BY, CC0 or PD in the sources searched.

## How I searched
- **Flickr** search HTML filtered to licences BY 2.0, CC0, PDM and BY 4.0. Queries: alaskan malamute, malamute (13 pages), malamutes (12 pages), malamute dog, malamute standing, malamute show, malamute stack, malamute dog show, alaskan malamute show dog, malamute gaiting, malamute trot, malamute walking, malamute profile, malamute side, malamute crufts, malamute westminster, malamute ring, malamute snow, malamute hiking, malamute weight pull, malamute kennel club, malamute puppy, sled dog (4 pages), freight sled dog, dog show working group, malamut, malamute de alaska, alaska malamut, alaskan malamut, chien malamute, malamute hund, malamute sled, mal dog, alaskan malamutes, malamute agility, malamute pulling, malamute world dog show. I also searched the Svenska Mässan photostream (malamute, spets, Grupp 5, BIR grupp 5).
  - About 550 unique results screened by contact sheet.
  - Most were not malamutes: "Malamute Drop Zone" US Army paratrooper photos, the "malamute08" snowboard event, sled-dog teams of Alaskan huskies, and puppies.
  - Licences of the candidates were checked on each Flickr photo page; all were licence id 4 (CC BY 2.0).
- **Openverse**: q=alaskan malamute, malamute (all sources, wikimedia, flickr), malamute dog show, malamute standing, snow, grass, leash, walk, side, portrait, show and malamutes. About 330 records; after removing Flickr duplicates, about 125 screened.
- **Wikimedia Commons**:
  - Category:Alaskan Malamute (109 files) and Category:Alaskan Malamute heads.
  - A full-text file search for "malamute" (408 files, mostly PDFs and military photos).
  - The licence of every plausible file was read from its file page: 89 files checked.
  - Commons originals return 429, so I downloaded cached thumbnails at 1920 px (or 1280 px). That is the full original for files narrower than 1920 px.
- **iNaturalist**: Canis familiaris (taxon 47144) with q=malamute or q=alaskan malamute, CC BY and CC0 photos only. 0 results.
- **US federal sources**:
  - The Denali NPS kennel dogs are Alaskan huskies (a sled-racing type), not malamutes, so they don't fit.
  - images.nps.gov is blocked by the egress proxy, and the NPS sled-dog page returned 404.
  - The PD historical photos on Commons were all too small (530–821 px) and show pre-breed "Mahlemut" freighting dogs. Examples: the Valdez c.1908 dog, Ben Downing's dog c.1899, and the Dawson sled teams.
- **Download note**: Flickr `_o` originals returned HTTP 429 all session. I downloaded the `_k` (2048 px) size instead, or `_h` (1600 px) when `_k` wasn't offered. For 479914734, only the 1024 px `_b` size was available.

## Breed judgement
- All kept dogs have malamute type: heavy bone, broad skull with a moderate stop, wedge-shaped muzzle, small wide-set ears, plume tail carried over the back, and brown eyes.
- Rejected as probable Siberian Husky, mix or "Alaskan husky" despite a malamute title:
  - Commons Alaskan_malamute_dog_at_sunset-other.jpg: lean, light-boned, tail hidden.
  - Flickr 2640715177 "The 2 Malamutes": lean black-and-white dog, partly behind a person.
  - Commons Alaska_malamute_-_Simon.jpg: blue eyes, which disqualify a malamute. It is also CC BY-SA.
  - Openverse rawpixel "Alaskan Malamute" o13 and o25: Siberian Husky type.
  - Malamute_in_Liarsville (CC BY 3.0, lying): pale and light-boned, breed doubtful.
- None of the kept dogs looks like an oversized "giant malamute". The show dogs are judged Best of Breed or group-placed breed-standard dogs.
- Flickr 53804339439, "Whiskey" (Library and Archives Canada, CC BY 2.0): a c.1900 freighting dog described as three-quarters wolf. It is not the modern breed, so I left it out.
