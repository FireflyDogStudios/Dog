## Compliance (what Shutter checked, beyond the agents' own checks)
- **Every licence re-read on its source page:** each Flickr photo page (licence id), each Commons file page (licence categories), the iNaturalist API (per-photo `license_code`) and the PhyloPic API.
- **Allowed licences only:** PD, CC0, CC BY 2.0, 3.0 and 4.0. **No BY-SA, NC or ND anywhere.**
- **Real animals:** every image checked by eye on contact sheets. No AI, composites or taxidermy.
- **File rules:** all files are under 5 MB, and every catalogue row has an author, a licence and a licence URL.
- **Removed:**
  - a Tarleton State University photo carrying a Public Domain Mark (a Texas state institution, so the basis is unclear);
  - a husky photo that is a scan of a show print (authorship unclear).
- **Flagged, kept:** three photos carry a **Public Domain Mark the photographer applied to their own work**. That is a statement, not a CC0 waiver. They are dingo `walking/01`, husky `head/04` and `golden-jackal/muscle-ref/01`. Swap them if you prefer.
- **Corrected:** husky `walking/01` is a front three-quarter view, not side-on, and husky `walking/02` is of doubtful breed. Both are noted in the catalogue.
- **Credit owed:** every CC BY image needs "author, licence" in `CREDITS` if anything derived from it ships. NPS and USFWS images are US federal works, so they are PD.
- **Sizes:** Wikimedia and Flickr originals were often rate-limited (HTTP 429). Many files are the largest cached size (1,920–2,048 px), and each row says so.

## Notes for whoever fetches next

- Flickr's search page can be read as HTML with a licence filter: `license=4,9,10,11`.
- The Flickr account ancientartpodcast.org re-uploads CC BY-SA Commons photos labelled as CC BY 2.0. Use the original licence.
