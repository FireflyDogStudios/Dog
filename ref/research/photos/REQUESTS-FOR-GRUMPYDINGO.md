## 1. National Park Service: straight-down wolf photos

**What we want:** a photo of one standing wolf taken from straight above (or very steeply above), so Firefly can measure body width. None exists in our set yet. The two closest are oblique aerial photos from Yellowstone where each wolf is only 200 to 400 px long.

**Be realistic:** aerial wolf surveys usually take angled photos, not straight-down ones, so a "no, we don't have those" is a likely answer. Asking costs nothing.

### Who to ask, and how
| Who | Why | How (checked on the NPS pages, Oct 7) |
|---|---|---|
| **Yellowstone National Park** (best bet) | The Yellowstone Wolf Project's photographers (for example Doug Smith and Dan Stahler) are NPS staff, and photos by NPS employees on duty are public domain | "Email Us" form on [nps.gov/yell/contacts.htm](https://www.nps.gov/yell/contacts.htm). They say replies take 5 to 10 business days. Phone 307-344-7381 (a recording only). Mail: Yellowstone National Park, P.O. Box 168, Yellowstone National Park, WY 82190-0168. For older archived wolf-project photos the park's research library is [yell_research_library@nps.gov](mailto:yell_research_library@nps.gov) (from the park's wolf-project records page) |
| Isle Royale National Park (long shot) | Runs wolf and moose surveys by plane | Form links on [nps.gov/isro/contacts.htm](https://www.nps.gov/isro/contacts.htm); phone 906-482-0984. **Caution:** the survey photos are taken by the Michigan Tech research team (credited to Rolf Peterson), not by NPS staff. They may be copyrighted, so they are not automatically public domain |

I could not find a dedicated photo-request form or email for the Wolf Project itself, so the Yellowstone "Email Us" form is the right door.

### What you can look at first (no request needed)
These are NPS photos that are already public domain:
- Yellowstone wolf photos: [nps.gov/media/photo/gallery.htm?id=25C97BD8-155D-451F-675E208BE082FE26](https://www.nps.gov/media/photo/gallery.htm?id=25C97BD8-155D-451F-675E208BE082FE26)
- Yellowstone's photo collection (about 13,000 images, "public domain, may be used at no charge", credit line "NPS Photo" or "NPS Photo by <name>"): [nps.gov/features/yell/slidefile/index.htm](https://www.nps.gov/features/yell/slidefile/index.htm)
- The two oblique aerial wolf photos already in our set: [Dan Stahler](https://www.flickr.com/photos/80223459@N05/49235371881/) and [Doug Smith](https://www.flickr.com/photos/80223459@N05/49234905848/) on Yellowstone's Flickr.

If they reply with files: put them in the repo folder `ref/research/photos/grey-wolf/top/` (or send them to Shutter), **and keep the email**. It is the proof of the licence. Shutter will catalogue them with the photographer's name and "public domain (NPS employee work)".

1. **A free rawpixel account.** You create it. Never share the password with anyone. Search reports disagree on the free daily download limit (5 to 10 a day), so the list below is in priority order, and some may wait until tomorrow.
2. **For each item you download:** (a) open the item page, (b) take a screenshot of the licence line near the download button (it should say CC0 or public domain), (c) download the largest file offered (the SVG for vector items, otherwise the PNG or JPG), and **don't edit it**.
3. **Send me the files and screenshots** in a chat attachment, or put them in the repo under `ref/research/photos/rawpixel-incoming/`. Shutter will then check each one and catalogue it. Anything whose page does not say CC0 or public domain is not used.
