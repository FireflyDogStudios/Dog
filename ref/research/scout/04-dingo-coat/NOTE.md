## Method for the suggested table (all EST)
- A dingo is a "short coat" dog: average body hair under 20 mm by the CC BY clinical definition.
- Fur depth taken as about 0.5-0.7 × hair length (wolf: depth 54-65 mm against 100-145 mm guard hairs; a short coat lies flatter, so the factor is applied to an assumed body hair of 15-25 mm).
- The longer guard hairs along neck, withers and back (UKC standard, and visible in the dingo photos in `fetched/10-coat-palettes`) get the largest offset; head, ears, front of the forelegs and lower legs the smallest.
- Summer ≈ 0.65 × winter (the UKC standard says the winter coat is "distinctively heavier"; no number exists).
