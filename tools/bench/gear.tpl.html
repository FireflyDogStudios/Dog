Den Gear Bench

Dingo gear, one piece at a time, made from the Smithy's own materials and drawn straight onto the hero's rig so you see it move. He keeps every piece he's been given on, in layer order (armor under, collar and jewellery over), so each new piece is judged against the ones already there. Every piece is a short code like a weapon.

Each chip is one material: base, shade, light, outline, exactly as the weapons use them. If a piece says Blackened, these are the Blackened colours it must be drawn in.

Notes

  • One rule for every piece: the band is the material of the whole piece, the fitting is its accent (buckle, studs, trim, rivets, edges). Pick Oak and Antler on every tab and everything he wears is oak with antler hardware.
  • Same forge, same materials, same brush. The band is a Smithy wrap, the fitting a Smithy fitting, the gem the Smithy's diamond with its rim and glint, and every shape is drawn the Smithy's way: outline, base, shade on the lower half, a light strip on the upper edge. The name is built the Smithy way too. No new palettes.
  • Collar: plain, studded, tag, rope. The gem is always centred on the band, studs spaced evenly either side; the buckle, tag or knot sits at the throat.
  • Tail guard: sleeve or guard, both in the band material with the fitting as the accent (trim on the sleeve; a metal edge and a rivet on each of the guard's four plates). An Oak guard matches an Oak collar. The guard's three gaps are the ring slots; the gem takes the middle one.
  • Tail rings: three slots in the guard's gaps, each ring its own piece: band material for the ring, fitting metal for its edges, its own gem, rarity and infusion. "Sync the three rings" (on by default) keeps material, metal, rarity and infusion matched while each ring keeps its own gem; turn it off to change one ring alone.
  • Body armor (the base layer): a silhouette of the body from just under the collar, over the back to the rump, down to a hem that stays above the leg roots, so the legs swing free beneath it. Hide (bound hem), Scale (rows of scallops edged in the fitting), Plated (a shoulder plate and a chest plate in the fitting metal). A girth strap behind the shoulder on all three. The gem rides the chest, clear of the near leg. The body cover will stack on top of this.
  • Layer order: body armor, tail guard, tail rings, then collar. Rings go over the guard and bracelets over any leg cover when they come.
  • Match across pieces: with it on, the band, fitting, gem, rarity and infusion you pick on one tab are used for every piece he wears, so you can judge a whole set in one material; style stays per piece.
  • Levels, stats and set bonuses are not here yet, by design: this is the drawing and the code.
