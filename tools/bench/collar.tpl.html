Den Collar Bench

The first piece of dingo gear: collars, made from the Smithy's own materials (its leather, cord and cloth wraps for the band, its brass, iron, silver and copper fittings, its gems, rarities and infusions) and drawn straight onto the hero's rig, over the collar he was drawn with. Every collar is a short code like a weapon. Pick parts, or reroll, and watch it move.

Notes

  • Same forge, same materials, same brush. The band is a Smithy wrap, the fitting a Smithy fitting, the gem the Smithy's diamond with its rim and glint, and every shape is drawn the Smithy's way: outline, base, shade on the lower half, a light strip on the upper edge. The name is built the Smithy way too. No new palettes.
  • Four styles: plain, studded, tag, rope (spikes and bell cut, Oct 3). The gem is always centred on the band, with the studs spaced evenly either side; the buckle, tag or knot sits at the throat. Rare and up glow.
  • It moves. The collar is attached to the body joint, so it bobs with him; later pieces attach to legs and tail the same way.
  • Levels, stats and set bonuses are not here yet, by design: this is the drawing and the code.
