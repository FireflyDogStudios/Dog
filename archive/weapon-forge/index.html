Weapon Forge Bench

Every weapon is built from stacked layers instead of recolours. Pick a shape, who made it, an infusion, a rarity and a variant, or roll one. Everything below is live CSS on the same emoji sheets the game already uses.

Layer 2 · makers

Same shape, five makers

Four of these are different emoji art sets that ship in the Den already. The fifth is a printed silhouette made from the flat set. None of them is a hue shift.

Layer 4 · rarity

The rarity ladder

Junk looks worn. Rare gets an outline. Epic adds a glint clipped to the blade's own shape. Legendary adds an aura, godly adds slow light rays, and mythic shifts its outline through colours.

Layer 3 · infusions

Infusions tell you the effect

The charm is hand-placed at each shape's grip and the sparks come off the striking end. Each one would map to a trait, so you can read what the weapon does at a glance.

Layer 5 · variants

Sunkissed and Starlit

Pet Sim style: fuse duplicates into a Sunkissed copy, then Sunkissed copies into a Starlit one, with a small power bump each step. The texture is clipped inside the art, so the drawing still shows through.

Layer 6 · signatures

One hand-made weapon per god

These are composed by hand, never generated. One per god, as the drop you remember.

Readability check

At bag size

Twenty random rolls at the size they'd sit in the bag. Small sizes drop the sparks and keep the charm, outline and glint.

Pool size

How big the pool gets

  • Shapes join the roll pool when you beat a zone's god, so the pool grows by milestones.
  • Each zone family keeps one maker, so areas stay visually consistent.
  • Names follow the Diablo pattern of prefix, shape and suffix, and the suffix comes from the weapon's trait.
  • Calm mode and reduced motion turn off every animation and keep the static outline and glow.
