Statuses (buffs and debuffs)

One shared list for the whole game. Boons have a moss edge, conditions a rosehip edge. The number is stacks, the thin line along the bottom is time left. Names are drafts. Your target's frame sits top-centre (the nearest creature, or the one you click). Your own statuses sit just above the weapon bar.

Where things live

Proposal: personal things open as windows anywhere. Places you visit hold the workshops, like GW2's banks and crafting stations.

Windows (anywhere)

  • Inventory: weapons, gems, tomes, brews, bait, materials
  • Pack: your companions and their gem sockets
  • Map: zones, caves, travel, trials
  • Journal: bestiary, trophies, wardrobe, relics
  • Mail, Wallet, Settings

The Den (home)

  • Library: study, research, scrolls, compendiums
  • Brewing nook: alembic and apothecary
  • Stash: the old vault
  • Trophy wall and decor
  • Sanctuary: where settled pups live

Town

  • Smithy: affix bench, gem sockets, salvage
  • Rescue Shelter: rescue pups
  • Meat Market (later)
  • Post Office: old letters and parcels

Out in the world

  • The Mine and caves: on the Map
  • Trial grounds: one per zone, on the Map
  • Events: in the tracker

Visiting the Den or Town pauses the hunt. Upgrades and Self Care are resting for now.
